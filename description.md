# entity: the subtree watch heal re-establishes live watches every minute; probe progress instead

The subtree watch pump in `entity/logdstore.go` re-establishes a watch after `subtreeHealAfter` (30s) of silence: it closes the stream and opens a new one, whose State resyncs the document and whose diff emits what was missed. The interval doubles only while nothing arrives, and any event resets it to 30s.

On staging that is the single largest source of load on logd. Versed holds ~391 engine pattern watches; most of their slices see an event about once a minute (ticks, repo-charters rounds), so each sees a 30s gap every minute and re-establishes about once a minute: a goroutine dump of logd shows ~1,400 watch streams of which 762 are under a minute old and none are 1–29 minutes old, i.e. ~13 opens a second, each paying `sendInitialState → Storage.Read` of the whole watched subtree (15% of logd's CPU in a 30s profile, ~30% of all its read work) and a decode and diff in versed. The old streams' teardowns time out under the load they cause ("unwatch of abandoned watch failed: context deadline exceeded", 61 in the pod's life). docd has one client, versed, so nothing else opens them.

The heal exists for a watch that dies silently; by its own comment that hole was closed upstream in v0.0.112 and the timer stays as a shape ("a consumer that can only ever be told is a consumer that waits forever whenever the telling is what breaks"). The safeguard is right; its cost is not. logd offers `Progress` on a watch, a small request the watch answers with its head, and versed already uses it for the perms mirror (`WatchSubtreeProgress`, `watermark.go`). At the heal, ask the watch for progress with a short deadline; an answer is proof of life and resets the timer, and only a watch that does not answer is re-established. The progress event the probe draws must not reach the consumer as a progress Commit it never asked for.

Expected on staging: the watch churn goes to zero while watches are alive, logd gets ~30% of its read work back, and the unwatch failures stop. The reads proper (matches) and the index walk they pay (tony-format issue on the Spine walk) are separate.
