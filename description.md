# runners: versed's token toward a runner is minted for that runner alone

On verse/verified-runners (pb6fwh8gh12krmb7vhn0) versed presented its projected workload token, audience `runner`, to every registration with a subject, before the runner had proved anything. The subject on the registration bounded who may be the runner; nothing bounded where a copy of versed's token was good: a copy taken at one runner, or sent to a mistyped url, opened every registered runner's door.

The per-runner audience closes it, and nothing new is typed: the subject on the registration is the audience. Versed is an issuer already (`auth.issuer`, the gate credential).

- versed mints the token it presents to a runner from `auth.issuer`: `iss` its issuer url, `sub` verse, `aud` the registration's subject, ten minutes, kept per registration until a minute before expiry. The default runner (`verse up -runner`) gets `aud: runner`. `verse runner add -subject` is refused where versed issues nothing, beside the refusal where it names no workload issuer. `verse up -token-file` and VERSE_TOKEN_FILE on versed are gone.
- the runner's block is `verse: {url, issuer, audience, ca}`: `url` where it reaches versed and reads its keys at `/.well-known/jwks.json` (oidc.NewRemoteKeySet + NewVerifier, no discovery document); `issuer` the `iss` it holds tokens to, default `url`; `audience` the `aud` it takes, default the subject of its own token (runner.TokenSubject), so a registered runner types nothing; `subject` is gone. 401 for a bearer that does not verify, 503 while the keys cannot be read. Env VERSE_URL, VERSE_ISSUER, VERSE_AUDIENCE, VERSE_CA.
- deploy: `runnerTokens` keeps the runners' ServiceAccounts and tokens and mounts the `verse-issuer` Secret (`issuer.pem`) into versed; knobs `issuer` (default `http://verse:9797`, the auth file's `auth.issuer.url`) and `issuerSecretKey`; `auth.issuer` in both auth files; VERSE_SUBJECT, workloadIssuer and workloadIssuerCA gone. Minikube makes the Secret by hand. Staging needs the `staging/verse/issuer` entry before the ship, which is one ship of versed and the runners together.

Built in 00974d097 on the branch. Design: the verified-runners page, "The runner's door" and "The per-runner audience". A prerequisite of seat runners (a runner an individual registers): the audience is the one thing that says "this token is for this runner".
