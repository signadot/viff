# a rule woken by a commit it cannot read at that moment never evaluates that commit again

Seen 2026-09-12 20:54Z, on the acme.drift drive. The git source reflected main moving from d1780aa5 to c1863dc9 (the merge train's push). acme.merge's train-cut fired on it (a train for c1863dc9 was derived). acme.drift's claim rule — cursor idle at d1780aa5, condition `sha: !not .[tip]` true — did not fire, and stayed unfired for over fifteen minutes with its watch goroutines alive (counts unchanged in a goroutine dump) and no throttle trip in the log. A no-op poke of the cursor (`verse data acme drift at --put '{poke: 1}'`) made it fire at once: covering c1863dc9, range derived, review run, finding resolved. So the rule was alive and its condition held; the wake on the head commit was lost.

Why: trigger/edge.go evaluate marks the commit seen (firstSight) and then reads the verse at that rev (`store.AsOf(c.Rev, …)`). A read that fails is traced at debug — `cannot read the verse at rev N; not firing` — and evaluate returns; firstSight has already recorded the rev, so the commit is never evaluated again, and a level rule whose subject is not what changed has nothing else to wake it until its reads move again. versed's log shows three store requests timing out at 20:54:22–20:54:24 (`dropping response with no matching request` ids 7490, 7496, 7505), exactly when the head commit landed: that was the read.

Same class as the startup phases fixed in 8abadb7c and the status listings in 756y35vzh12kswn6mnn0: a read that did not happen taken as an answer. Here the answer taken is 'nothing selected', and the trace is at debug.

What it should do: a read that did not happen (entity.IsUnread, or any AsOf error) is not a verdict — give the sight back (or do not take it until the read succeeds) and evaluate the commit again after a pause, bounded by the rule's context, saying so at Warn once. The subtree watch's own heal (entity/logdstore.go subtreeHealAfter) re-establishes a dead watch and diffs; it does not help here, because the watch delivered and the engine dropped the commit on its own read.
