# runners: versed's token toward a runner is minted for that runner alone

On verse/verified-runners (pb6fwh8gh12krmb7vhn0) versed presents its projected workload token, audience `runner`, to every registration with a subject, before the runner has proved anything. A copy taken at one runner, or sent to a mistyped url, opens every registered runner's door. The subject on the registration bounds who may be the runner; nothing bounds where a copy of versed's token is good.

The per-runner audience closes it, and nothing new is typed: the subject on the registration is the audience. Versed is an issuer already (`auth.issuer`, the gate credential). Three commits, on the branch before its first ship so staging sees one shape:

1. `versed!: the token toward a runner is minted for that runner alone`. On every call to a registration with a subject versed mints from `auth.issuer`: `iss` the issuer's url, `sub` `verse`, `aud` the registration's subject, ten minutes, cached per registration until a minute before expiry. The default runner (`verse up -runner`) gets `aud: runner`, which only a runner configured to accept that audience takes. `verse runner add -subject` is refused where versed is no issuer, beside the refusal where it names no workload issuer. `SetRunnerToken`, `verse up -token-file`, `VERSE_TOKEN_FILE` on versed go. Tests: a token minted for one registration refused at another's door; the default runner's audience; the two refusals at add.
2. `runnerd!: a runner holds its verse's tokens to its own name`. `verse: {url, issuer, audience, ca}`: `url` where it reaches versed and fetches discovery and keys; `issuer` the `iss` it holds tokens to, default `url`; `audience` the `aud` it accepts, default the `sub` of its own token file; `subject` goes. The guard uses `oidc.InsecureIssuerURLContext` where `issuer` and `url` differ. Env `VERSE_URL`, `VERSE_ISSUER` (versed's name), `VERSE_CA`; `VERSE_SUBJECT` gone. Tests: the guard against versed's own discovery route with `issuer` naming something other than the url; no token file and no `audience` refused at start.
3. `deploy: versed is an issuer, and its runners hold its tokens to their own names`. Knob `issuer` (versed's url outside the cluster), Secret `verse-issuer-key` versed mounts (ExternalSecret on staging as `verse-codex`, generated on minikube), `auth.issuer: {url, key}` in both auth files, `VERSE_ISSUER` in the runner patches from the knob, versed's ServiceAccount token and `VERSE_SUBJECT` removed. Minikube's `issuer` is `http://verse:9797`. Rollout: one ship of versed and the runners together.

The issuer url is not a constraint: a relying party may fetch discovery from one url and hold tokens to another `iss` (OIDC's allowance for an issuer behind a proxy), so the runner fetches in-cluster and expects versed's outside name.

Design: the verified-runners page, "The per-runner audience" and "Next, in order". A prerequisite of seat runners (a runner an individual registers): the audience is the one thing that says "this token is for this runner".
