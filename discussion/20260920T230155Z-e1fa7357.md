## Item 1: overlap reads a descent now; containment does not, and that is the remaining half

`602d68c0`. `entity.Overlap` is the walk, with `descends` passed in beside `disjoint` so each
pattern kind says which of its segments is the descent — `PathIsDescend` for a charter's
subject, `kp.Descend` for a kinded path, `NoDescent` for a source's claim.

**The upstream half was already done.** `17hj5ygk` closed and verse is on go-tony v0.0.229, so
`Matches` agrees with `patternReaches` about a descent. This item was the last of the three.

**What the oracle found.** The test enumerates every address over a small alphabet, reads both
patterns with a brute-force matcher written out separately, and requires the walk to agree on
all 7225 pairs of patterns up to length three. It disagreed with two rows of the hand-written
table, and the hand-written rows were the wrong ones: `a..y` and `a.b.z` DO meet, because
`a.b.z` is above `a.b.z.y` and the descent reaches it. The ancestor rule makes overlap far more
permissive than it reads, which is worth knowing before anyone writes a descending stratum and
is surprised by what it collides with.

**Containment is a different question and is unchanged.** `matchContains` (stratum check #1,
`Matches.contains`) uses `entity.Under`, which is still positional. On a descent it answers
false, which REFUSES — the safe direction, and the opposite of overlap's failure. So it is
sound and incomplete rather than wrong, and nothing had to change to keep it safe.

Making it complete is language inclusion rather than intersection: "every address the inner
reaches, the outer reaches too", with `..` on both sides. That is a harder problem than the
walk that just landed and wants its own thinking — not a variation on it. Until it is done, a
descending stratum will be refused where it should be allowed, which is an annoyance rather
than a hazard.

**Still on this item, then:**

- containment (`entity.Under` / `segContains`) reading a descent;
- the surfaces that refuse `..` through `ExprSegs`, each gated on the covering check its own
  kind uses: a suspension's subject (`trigger/suspend.go:155`), a grant's `at`
  (`entity/grant.go:288`), a grantee (`entity/grantee.go:119`), a source's claim
  (`source/own.go:196`);
- `entity.PatternSegs` still has no callers — it is the descent-accepting parser those
  surfaces would switch to.
