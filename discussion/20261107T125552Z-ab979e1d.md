**The text of a post is not in its step either, and with 275art3x that is the next gap.**

275art3x makes an answer perform only the act whose step it was given about. A Slack post's step
is `slack.post` with `channel` alone (and `thread` for a reply), so once this issue's fix names the
decision, an approval for one post in `deploys` still performs any text the task's process then
sends to `deploys` under that decision. The same for a reply's text and a reaction's emoji, which
the step does carry (`emoji`), so a reaction is bound and a post is not.

What would close it, as a follow-on rather than in this fix:

- `text` as a field of `slack.post` and `slack.reply` (form `text`), read by one function on both
  sides, so a row can decide a post by its words and an answer is bound to them; or
- a digest of the text in the step, where a long text should not be carried whole in a question.

Which, and with what bound, is the same question 1v5art3x leaves open for an MCP call's long
arguments; settling it once for both is the reason to note it here.
