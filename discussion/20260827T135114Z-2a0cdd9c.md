FIXED (`e3f4c400`), and there is NO logd defect — the store was ruled out with measurements rather than left as a suspicion.

## What it actually was

A question and the resolver instance beside it are TWO WRITES: `mirror.Watch` mints the instance when the question is posed. Eight test sites built an instance id and addressed it the instant `waitQuestion` returned, racing that second write.

Every route under `/resolvers/instance/{id}` answers **404** until the mint lands — so the race presented as "no such instance" rather than as a wait, which is exactly why it read as a store fault. One sibling test already documented the race and waited (`TestWatchingAgainDoesNotResetAPosition`: "Wait for the MINT, not just the question"); the others did not.

`waitOwner` had the same shape and was already named for a thing it did not do — it waited for the QUESTION, then returned whatever `resolver` said at that instant, which is `""` for an owner arriving a millisecond later. It waits for the owner now.

## Two more product defects found on the way

`instanceOf` — which every `/resolvers/instance/{id}` route hangs off — folded a failed read into "no such instance", so an unreadable store tells a resolver its own read plane is gone. `resolverVerdict`'s question read did the same. Both 503 now. These are the two sites the `err != nil || !ok` grep in 4f0026df missed: they spell it `!found`.

## What was ruled out, and how

- **Read-your-write**: 300 write-then-read pairs on one session, 480 concurrent across 8 writers, 300 across TWO sessions over one substrate. **Zero missed.** The store is sound on the thing this looked like.
- **`Get`'s three absent paths** instrumented separately (`not_found`, nil node, the `keyStands` tiebreak). The not-founds under load were all legitimate — polls before a write, and prior tests' leaked goroutines reading their own closed stores.
- **`mirror.Watch` discards its write result** (`_, _, _ =`), so a failed mint would be silent. Instrumented under load: it never failed, and when the create-only precondition refused, the entity always genuinely stood there. Worth tightening on its own terms, but it is not this.

So the "reads that succeeded and answered not-found for something that was written" from my previous comment was wrong: nothing had been written yet. I was reading the 404 as evidence about the store when it was evidence about the mint.

## Result

The loaded reproduction went **6 failures in 6 runs → 0 in 8**. Unloaded, it had been failing 1 run in 4; now 4x clean, plus `make test` twice and `./smoke.sh` green.

Leaving this open or closing it is your call — the flake is gone, but the deadline/override race `7b6cb09d`'s discussion describes (two writers, a precondition evaluated against a lagging view) was never the thing failing here and is still unaddressed.
