**A `then` may answer another ask, or ask one** (2026-09-30). The use case: `ship` needs two proceeds, and my ballot waits on Bob's yes that the UI is ok.
- **The two new kinds:** `then: {answer: {ask, choice, say?, force?}}` and `then: {ask: {…}}`.
- **Authority:** both are made on a yes, as the asker, on the asker's authority alone. The yes is only the condition, and lends the asker nothing.
- **Consent is to the wording:** an answer to a person's ask is tied to its version when the asking is raised, so if that ask is reworded before the yes, the answer is refused.
- **Chains:** they nest through the inner ask's `then`. A `due` of a duration counts from the yes that raises the inner ask.
