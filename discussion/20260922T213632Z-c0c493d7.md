## No buttons

Decided 2026-09-22: the buttons come out altogether, and the message is prose with a footer saying
how to answer in words.

They read as cheap, and they were always the narrow path: a block renders in fewer places than a
notification does, five options are five mystery ids in a row, and a message that carries both
buttons and a typed footer teaches two ways of doing one thing.

What this changes in the steps above: option prose (1) is for the footer and the record rather
than for a label; the layout (4) has no actions block; answering by reply (5) is the only way to
answer from Slack rather than the primary one. What survives the removal is the half that matters
and that (5) needs unchanged — a Slack id becoming `user:<email>` through users.info, the answer
made as that person, and what became of it said to them alone.

The gap it opens, deliberately: between the removal and (5) working live — which needs the app's
Event Subscriptions switched on — nothing in Slack answers a gate, and `verse gate approve` is
the path. So the removal and (5) land together.
