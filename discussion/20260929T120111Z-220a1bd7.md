**Stage 5 resolved: there is no policy mechanism.** A resolver is a process holding a party's grant, so its policy is its code. It reads the ask's clock and may answer at deadline minus one, which is how it overrides silence-as-yes. "Only the resolver answers" is the party choosing not to. What is left is how a party and their resolver share the party's answer. It is the party's to manage:

- **Answering:** a party who answers as themselves an ask a resolver serves for them gets 409, naming the resolver, unless they pass `force` (`--force`). The same holds for a place's owner and a resolver they granted at the place. It is checked against grants and match as they stand when the answer arrives.
- **The docket** (`verse gate`, `GET /gates`) leaves out asks a resolver serves for you. `--served` includes them, marked with the resolver.
- **The ask watch** shows them too, marking which ones are served and by which resolver. It asks `GET /asks/{id}/served` when an ask starts waiting on you.
- **Not done:** nothing stops a resolver from overwriting a ballot its party forced in. That is the party's to manage.
