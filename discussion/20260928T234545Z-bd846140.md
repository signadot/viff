## The model: "Changing an ask"

A section added to the model, next to Sequences. It builds on the rendezvous's extension (scenario 7): the raiser changes an ask while it waits.

### Changing an ask

An ask that is still waiting is changed by its raiser, through the verb that owns it. After it settles, nothing changes: anything more is a new ask.

- **The clock** (extended, or shortened while still in the future) changes without asking again. Consent was given to what was asked, not to when.
- **What was asked** (words, options, `then`, parties) asks again. Consent was given to what was posed, so a change to it needs new consent.
- **Answers belong to a revision.** An ask carries a revision, and each answer and ballot names the revision it answered. Asking again drops the ballots cast against an earlier revision, so a quorum half gathered is gathered again.
- **Resolvers see a change on their subscription,** like any other event. When what was asked changes, the resolver serves the ask again. When the clock changes, it may move the clocks of its onward asks, which still end no later than the ask they serve.
- **Linked asks follow.** When what was asked changes, onward asks raised for an earlier revision are withdrawn, and the resolver raises new ones if it still needs them. A clock change leaves them standing.
