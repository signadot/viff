**What this changes in the runner contract.**

- `Launch.Autonomy` and `Launch.Gates` → `Launch.Policy`: ordered rows `{name, match: {system, verb, <params>}, then: deny|ask|allow|withhold|{ask: {need, answerers, within, default, labels}}}`, first match wins, the last row the default. `withhold` names a system or a verb, never a call: the tool is not offered, fixed at launch; a replacement policy (`PUT /tasks/{id}/policy`, `{policy, by}`) withholds exactly what the task started withholding, and keeps the rows on any system the offer holds `provisioned` or `none`. The runner reads `need`, `answerers`, `within`; `default` and `labels` are verse's.
- `With.Repos` → `With.Git` (`{repo, from}`) and `With.GitIssue` (`{repo}`). `With.Network` is gone: what a task may reach is the policy's `allow` rows on `network.tcp-client` (`host`, `port`; a host is a name or `*.<domain>`); a row with no host is the runner's own network; no row is no network. The push allowance → rows over `git.push` (`repo, ref, delete, force`) and `github.create-pr`/`update-pr` (`repo, base, head`).
- `Offer`: `agent|script`, `with` (the doer's own parameters: `model`, `effort`), `annotations`, `converse`, and `systems: {<name>: {holds: policed|provisioned|none, ceiling: [rows]}}`. No `intercepts`, `forges`, `credentials` or `with.git`: a system offered is a system held. The `git` ceiling is completed within the forge (the operator's rows, their default for `<host>/**`, deny for the rest). `files`, `commands` and `agent` are held policed for the doers the runner stops them on; `network` is provisioned under a sandbox that can take the network away, `none` otherwise.
- Install and slide: allow and ask rows must fall within the ceilings, and a refusal names both rows. A row on a system the offer does not hold is refused ("holds no <system>"); `ask` on a provisioned or unheld system is refused; under `none` the policy must say `{match: {system: <name>}, then: allow}` and nothing narrower.
- At the act: the delivery server re-reads the ceiling for the systems it performs; the supervisor reads the runner's ceilings after the policy for the ones inside the run (`ByCeiling`).
- Tool names are `<system>-<verb>`, steps `<system>.<verb>`; the documents live at `verse.systems.<name>`; a runner claims its verbs and checks them at start. New verbs: `agent.call-sub-agent`, `network.tcp-client` (`host`, `port`).
- Events: pending, decision and exec carry `step` and `fields`; a decision's `gate` is the row. The task record and `GET /tasks/{id}` carry `held` (system → how it was held).
- Provider selection is gone (8a66e0ad): no `provider:` on a spawn, no `selected` on the record, no `verse provider`. A spawn names its doer, and its `runner` when not the default.

**For a charter author.** Every charter in the tree and in `deploy/` carries `{match: {system: network}, then: allow}`. `verse task run` writes that row itself (f2f8b489); `--network none` or `--network <host[:port]>,…` says less.
