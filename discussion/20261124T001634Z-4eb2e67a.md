Worked on branch `task-end-6-follow-ups`, on top of `task-end-5-runner-lease` (`2f6e3d52`). Five commits, `d2cf955d..fb13c927`, not pushed.

- **8, the checks that moved off the charter lock** (`d2cf955d`)
  - An uninstall of one package, by request or at its ttl, asks whether it may and locks the package under one hold of the charter lock (`startUninstall`). A renewing install after the ttl's re-read is refused with 429, not taken and then removed.
  - `RemoveTriggerAs` is refused once the engine closes, and is counted as work, so `Close` waits for a removal that is under way. `RemoveTrigger`, the host's, is not refused.
  - The two handlers that call `RemoveTriggerAs` now answer its error instead of falling through.
- **10, the 1m floor** (`785678ad`). `task.Accepting.SubscribeAccepted` takes a wait. The mirror waits a quarter lease at most for each subscribe: 15s at the 1m floor.
- **11** (`d2cf955d`, `785678ad`, `9a027111`, `fb13c927`)
  - The charter routes' 429 and 503 answers carry `Retry-After`, and a charter lock that is not got in time is answered 503.
  - `verse task attach` says when the record could not be read.
  - A launch that joins a running task renews its lease, and sets it to the one the launch names. A shorter lease takes effect at once.
  - `landEnd` says the end has not landed again when the reason changes (once a minute at most) and every 5 minutes, and says when the end lands.
  - The wording on hooks during an uninstall is qualified: a hook that would start work is refused.
  - The three tests asked for are added: an unreachable runner during an uninstall, the old stream closed on a resubscribe, and a heal before the wait that replays the runner's own `failed`.
- **A viewer past a lease** (`785678ad`)
  - The subprocess runner no longer byte-proxies a task's events. It serves them frame by frame through its client to the task's process.
  - A stream open when the task gets its last word (the lease, a cancel before the task is hosted, the process gone) ends with that word, after the frames the runner followed past the reader's cursor.
  - A terminal frame from the process goes to a reader only as the end that stands. Once given out, it counts as the task's end, so the lease then ends nothing.
- **`dropSystemAddress`** (`a42abef9`). It removes nothing on a listing that was not read.

Every new test fails with its fix disabled. Two exceptions: the heal test and the closed-stream check cover behaviour that already existed, as asked.

Gates: `make check` is clean. The full suite is green except `TestEveryDeployedCharterInstalls`, which fails on `main` too. The smoke passed alone, 67 checks.
