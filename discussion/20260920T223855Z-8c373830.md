## Item 2: `entity.Depth` is gone; what is left is narrower than this issue predicted

`236d1f44` deletes the constant. The ratchet falls 42 → 30, and `entity/read_test.go` and
`versed/packages.go` leave `scripts/check-paths.baseline` entirely.

**The dependency ran the other way.** This item said "Deleting it deletes `entity.Depth`" —
that `List` had to go first. It did not: nine call sites spelled a registry's own depth as
`entity.Depth+1`, and each could state its own number without anything changing about `List`.
Charter records are two below `verse.charter`, perms records one below the mirror — which is
what `ChildOf` already said at the top of `attribution.go`, three thousand lines above the
listing that had to spell it as a depth from the root. `entity.Below`/`BelowErr` took all of
them. The ninth was not a depth question at all: `gateID` sized a slice by
`len(subjects)*Depth`, a capacity hint computed from an arity nothing enforces.

**What survives is `walkEntityDoc`'s arity, renamed `listWalkArity`** so it stops looking
general. It is stuck for a reason worth stating: `List` names no address, so there is nothing
to count FROM. Every other walk could state a depth relative to its own place once it had a
place to state it about; this one guesses, and three is the guess.

**The production caller this item named is already gone.** It said `List` was kept alive by "a
listing of everything for a claim sweep (`versed/sources.go`) and about thirty tests". The
claim sweep now goes through `source.List`, which is a subtree read — as does `runner.List`,
each with a comment about a `List(pred)` being a Match on the root. The only non-test
references left in the tree are the implementations themselves:

    entity/doc.go:674          out := r.List(pred)            // the ListErr fallback
    entity/attribution.go:1628 a.keepAll(a.Store.List(pred))
    entity/attribution.go:1699 n.a.keepAll(n.Snapshot.List(pred))

So this item is no longer a design question with a production cost. It is: delete `List(pred)`
and the walk behind it, move ~32 test call sites to `ListAt`/`Below`, and `listWalkArity` goes
with them. Nothing in production has to change shape for it.

**A gap in the ratchet, worth knowing before trusting the number.** `check-paths.sh` matches
`entity\.Depth` and `^const Depth = ` — qualified uses and the definition. It never counted the
three in-package uses in `entity/attribution.go` and `entity/logdstore.go`, which are only
visible from inside package `entity`. They were found by the compiler after the constant was
deleted, not by the check. Any in-package successor would be invisible to it the same way.

Item 1 (overlap does not read a descent) and items 3–4 are untouched.
`docs/reference/entities.md`'s pending block now names `listWalkArity` and says why it is
stuck, rather than saying "a fixed `Depth` also survives".
