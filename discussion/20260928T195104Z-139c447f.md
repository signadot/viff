Step 0, the probe. Worktree `.worktrees/perms-order` (branch `verse/perms-order` at 99336508, nothing committed). The probes are kept in `scratchpad/probe_permsorder_test.go.txt`. go-tony v0.0.239.

**1. The complete revision sequence: no.**
- A logd watch delivers only commits that change its path. The server tracks the commits it skips (`accountFor`, logd/server/session_watch.go:346) but never reports them, and it has no progress event. `PongResult.Commit` is the store's head, not a watch's delivered-through point.
- Probe: commits 7 (a same-value write) and 8 (a scope) reached no baseline watch.
- A single watch on `verse` still gives a sound lower bound, because it delivers in commit order. But it stalls for an R it never delivers, and indefinitely on an idle store.
- Load, 5000 writes by 8 writers: one raw consumer on `verse` got all 5000, lag p99 6–17ms, max 55ms. A slow consumer (1ms per event) is ended loudly by logd (`slow_consumer`) after 316 events, with a commit to resume from.
- **So the watermark needs a barrier** for an R the stream doesn't carry: a fresh mirror read at a commit ≥ R, or a marker write.

**2. The revision a read was served at: yes in libctl, dropped by verse.**
- `MatchCommit` / `MatchPatternCommit` (libctl/logd.go:481) and `MatchEach*` (logd_set.go:88) return the commit the read was served at. Paging stays at that commit.
- verse discards it in `Get` (entity/logdstore.go:541), `ReadAt` (:1256), `ListErr` (:1214), `ListAtErr` (:1637, which `BelowErr` goes through) and `PathsAt` (:1335).
- The commit returned is the head the read saw, not the path's last change. 0 of 300 concurrent reads reported a commit below a write that had already returned.
- **So R is the read's own commit:** exact, and one changed call per read.

**3. The mirror at a past revision: yes, but exact only above the replay floor.**
- `MatchAt(verse.verse.perms.node, R)` answered correctly at old and recent revisions. Cost, with 101 records and 4.1k commits: 0.5–1.5ms, about a current read.
- verse compacts after 1h (cmd/verse/docd.go:249). Below the floor, `MatchAt` answers the nearest earlier snapshot's state silently, and answered not_found for a record that stood then (storage/replay_floor.go:22-40).
- Replays below the floor are refused (`replay_compacted`), so every event a replay can deliver is above it.
- **So `MatchAt` can back the view for any servable R, but it must never answer for an R at or below the floor.** There an absent record lets the grants above decide, which fails open. Through docd, the floor is learned only from a refusal.

**Found on the way, separate from this issue:** `entity.LogdStore.Observe` loses commits silently once it falls behind. Under 5000 writes it lost 2392 commits in two contiguous runs, all with `Gap=0`. `runWatch` (entity/logdstore.go:2440) re-establishes from State after a slow-consumer end without replaying, and `handleEvent` (:2472) patches the whole verse document for each event. Its `Gap` counts only drops at the 256-slot channel. That's outside `Observe`'s "may drop, with a gap" contract. Not filed.
