**It builds only against the unreleased go-tony.** An uncommitted `replace` in `go.mod` points at the gkmdvps7 checkout (1c36aa2c). A go.work made `TestPinnedGoTonyIsReadable` fail on "(devel)"; that test skips for a replace. The pin gets bumped when go-tony releases.

**Mechanism**
- A watermark per view: how far the records watch and the root-record watch have applied.
- Reads report the commit they were served at, and watch events carry their own.
- Below R, one coalesced `Progress` goes to both perms watches. The view advances on the progress events that come back in order.
- It fails closed on an error, including `unsupported` or a re-established watch, and when the answer misses its budget.
- The mirror is read only to load the view, after a watch is re-established, or for a store whose watches can't be asked.
- The perms watches are plain docd base paths. No `unsupported` in any test through docd.
