Reopened. 5e1af4c6c and 998e35c6c, on `verse/owned-rules`, are not a fix to land. The `Grants` write they use asks its precondition at `verse`, which evaluates it over the whole verse half. And 9183958c5 exists only because rules were moved onto that path.

The mechanism, as read so far:
- Under a follow, an install is placed by the follow, not by its own admit (`followed`).
- In subtree mode, `admit` holds the rule with `causePass` and kicks a pass.
- That pass's `try` vets the rule with `e.OwnerOf(name)`. When it runs before the owner record lands (the take, or a person's post-commit record), it answers `""` and arms the rule as nobody.
- The kick after the take (trigger_api.go:85) only re-vets rules that are still held.

The design that governs this is in 0c807e51c, "a place that acts is installed as somebody's, or not at all":
- `resolve` stays on a place's own record.
- An install checks who the place acts as (`actsAsInstaller`).
- A pass is meant to re-vet the rule as whose it is.

Holding a recordless rule in `try` was reviewed and is not enough on its own:
- An install's `passed` would stop waiting on a rule held as nobody, and answer "held".
- Scope engines are not kicked by a baseline install.
- A mirror read error reads as nobody.
- A record landing without a kick waits up to `reconcileEvery`, one minute.
