**Decided (2026-09-29), resolvers: a match does not route.** This replaces the Q2 answer above.
- A resolver's match names which asks it follows, by what they are and what they're about. It never names whom they're put to, or who raised them.
- Which parties a resolver answers for comes only from their grants. A person grants a resolver to answer for them; a place's owner grants one for asks about the place.
- Nobody can take another's asks by declaring a match, since a match gives no authority to answer. A person's delegation and a place's resolver don't overlap, because neither is a routing claim.
- The overlap refusal applies to matches over what asks are about, as it does to a source's slice.
