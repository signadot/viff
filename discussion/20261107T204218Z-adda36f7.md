**Closing, 2026-11-07: the fix is on main and the issue was left open.** `bd5b7271` ("docket: a task's question says what the action is") is on `origin/main` and every branch since: `task.ToolUse.Describe()` (`pkg/task/action.go`), carried on `Pending.Action`, the `pending` and `decision` frames, the snapshot, the question record and `attach`'s prompt. The record has since gained the step itself: `api.AskRecord` holds `tool`, `action`, `step` and `fields` (the step's fields, each in its form's type), and `verse question <id>` prints the record as it stands, so an answerer at the CLI sees the step whole.

What it left, and is now its own issue: the Slack ask (`pkg/io/slack/out.go`) renders a question's `pose` and `about` only, never `action` or `fields`, as this issue's build comment said. And `Describe()` leaves nested values out by design, so a step whose arguments are nested (an `mcp.call`'s `spec`) is seen whole only through `fields`.
