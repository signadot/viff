Decisions for step 6 (2026-10-21):

- **Split in two, each green.**
  - **6a:** ceilings as positive blocks with their own type, `systems.Ceiling`.
    - Attributes hold lists of patterns; a single value is shorthand for a one-entry list. A step may match more than one block.
    - Strict containment at install (on the spec's own environment, where a `$` attribute is unknown and passes), at launch (on the bound environment), and at the act (defence in depth; a refusal there is logged as a bug).
    - The row-vs-ceiling check goes, and `withinForge`/`ceilingOf` go with it. A `git` ceiling block outside the forge is refused when the config is read.
    - Config: `systems.<name>: {credential?, api?, ceiling}`.
    - The deploy ceilings are rewritten fresh, since there is no "everything but" form, and the shipped charters are checked against them.
    - A system held with no ceiling is closed.
  - **6b:** the offer reshape.
    - `agent: {name, model: [patterns], effort, annotations}` or `script: true`; `model` absent means any. The default model stays private, and the record says which model ran.
    - One rule for the default agent: the first agent offer.
    - `with`/`Accepts` go.
- **Physical facts are published beside the ceiling, never merged into it:** `systems.<name>: {supports: <blocks>, ceiling: <blocks>}`.
  - `supports` is what the runner's nature can realize of the document, derived, never written in config. For now that is only the network's `mode`, from the sandbox.
  - It shares the ceiling's block form (`mode: [open]`).
  - An absent `supports` is the whole document; an absent `ceiling` is closed. The network is the exception: an absent network ceiling means no bound beyond `supports`.
  - An environment sits within the document, within `supports` and within the ceiling, each refused in its own words. `supports` lands in 6b.
