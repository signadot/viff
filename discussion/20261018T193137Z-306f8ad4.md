Made (each its own commit; `Checked:` says which have a regression test):
- **Param forms** in the system documents: bool, ref, branch, upper, lower, repo.
  - A pattern that no covered verb's form takes is refused, as is a null param.
  - A `ref` or `repo` pattern spells out its slashes or uses `**`.
  - A step's `repo` is lowercase `host/owner/name`.
  - An ask's `default` is proceed or deny.
- **Rows and written policies:** row names are unique, and `turn`, `ask` and `question-ask` are reserved. A policy a person writes is read strictly.
- **Questions:** a row's question takes its clock, default and need from the row. A question already on record keeps its options, clock, answerers and labels.
- **Replacements and ceilings:** a replacement is held to the launch's verbs and its withheld set. A ceiling allows or denies, nothing else.
- **Virtual key:**
  - `verse up`'s runner takes `verse.portkey/virtual-key` again. This was a regression from main: every firing that named a `virtualKey` failed.
  - `doer.annotations` may not name the key.
  - Install sees the key, and a templated repo keeps its owner at install.
- **Resumed tasks:** the record keeps prompt, doer, git, credential, occurrence and started. A refused spawn mints no credential.
- **git-issue:**
  - The mirror fetch prunes.
  - A delete is leased.
  - A close at the remote is pulled as a move.
  - A tracker needs an offer that holds git-issue.
- **Allow entries:** a host is written as the proxy compares it, and an address in Go's short form.
- **Codex:** a permission kind or a file-system access that this side does not read is gated as a write.
