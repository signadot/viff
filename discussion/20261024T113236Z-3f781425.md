## Code-level plan

The model is `github-issue`'s comment and close (runner/runnerd/delivery/issues.go `githubIssue`): a step decided by the autonomy and the ceiling, performed with the repository's credential, answered as text.

**runner/runnerd/delivery/protocol.go**

**runner/runnerd/delivery/github.go**
- `OpGitHubListPRs = "github.list-prs"`, `OpGitHubReadPR = "github.read-pr"`.
- `ListGitHubPRs(ctx, hc, token, api, owner, repo)`: `GET /pulls?state=open&per_page=50&page=n`, paging by counter until a short page, to a cap of 500, and returning whether the cap cut it. A counter pager, as io/github's `listReviews` does: nothing in delivery pages today, and io/github is not importable here (it drags in client and mirror).
- `ReadGitHubPR(ctx, hc, token, api, owner, repo, n)`: `GET /pulls/{n}` and `GET /pulls/{n}/reviews`, paged the same way.
- `githubAPI` reads at most 1 MB and fails opaquely past it; a listing item carries whole repository objects, so 100 pulls with bodies can exceed it. The paged reads use a variant with a 4 MB limit that reports a page over the limit as such. Omitting bodies from the OUTPUT does not shrink what GitHub sends.
- Decode structs `ghPR` (number, title, state, draft, user, base/head ref, updated_at, created_at, html_url, body, requested_reviewers, requested_teams) and `ghReview` (user, state, submitted_at).
- The text the task reads: one line per pull request for a list (`#123 title — author, updated …, requested: a, b; teams: x`), and for a read the fields, then the reviews one per line, then the body last, introduced as written by the author.
- A missing pull request (404) is a refusal, not an error: permanent, and the task re-plans on it, as `slackFailed` treats a permanent refusal.

**runner/runnerd/delivery/server.go**
- `answer` dispatches the two ops to `s.githubRead(ctx, tk, req)`, `githubIssue`'s shape: the tree, `s.Ceilings["github"]` present, `fields` = `{repo}` plus `{pr}` for a read, `s.refused`, `RepoPath`, `tk.Credential`, `s.http()`, then the call. A `pr` that is not a number is refused as a bad issue number is.
- `answer` holds no mirror lock for these; the 10 min `ForgeTimeout` covers the pages.

**runner/runnerd/verserunner/repotools.go**
- `listPRsTool`, `readPRTool` = `systems.Tool("github", …)`.
- `repoTools` appends `prReadTool(cl, ws, all, listPRsTool)` and `prReadTool(…, readPRTool)` inside `if github`.
- `prReadTool` is `githubIssueVerb`'s shape: params `repo` (enum) and `pr` for a read; `Fields` gives `repo` canonical and `pr` as `strconv.Itoa(n)`, the server uses `req.PR` as sent, so an `ask` row's answer matches by the same keys and spellings; `Do` sends `Decision: tu.Decision` (as `githubIssueVerb` and `prTool` do; `slackTool`'s Do does not, so it is not the one to copy).
- The descriptions say the title, body and reviews are other people's words and come into the context as written.

**All four doers get them with no change of their own:** runner.go:204-266 builds one `tools` list, filtered by `offered(tools, l.Environment)`, and hands it to the script task, codex, claude (served over its MCP server) and the verse agent (`Extra`). **Ceilings:** `given` (systems/environment.go:364) is shared by environments and ceilings: a block naming no verbs gives every verb of the document. The three staging runners' `github: {ceiling: {repo: "github.com/signadot/**"}}` therefore grant the new verbs as they land, and install's `ContainsAsWritten` holds a charter block `github: [{repo: X, list-prs: {}, read-pr: {}}]` when a `git` block checks X out; no deploy change. The same holds for an environment: `github: [{repo: …}]` with no verbs would also give `create-pr`, so the alert charter names its verbs.
