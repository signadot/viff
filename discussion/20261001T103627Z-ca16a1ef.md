For the charter trial: ios do not read scopes today. A sink, such as the Slack io posting a message, does not see the writes of a scope.

What follows for ambiguity 3 (effects) and 4 (report):

- **Sink effects of a trial are inert today.** A trial charter that writes a Slack message, or any other sink entity, writes it into the scope's layer, and no io carries it out. A trial does not post by accident.
- **The same writes are the trial's report material.** What the scope wrote to sinks is what the candidate *would have sent*. It sits in the scope's layer, and can be diffed against what main's charters sent over the same events.
- **What a trial needs to reach the world deliberately is not possible today.** Examples are posting its report into the review environment, or asking a trial gate in Slack. Either an io reads a named scope, chosen on purpose per io rather than for all of them, or the system charter (in baseline) carries the scope's output across.
- **Not covered:** a task spawned in the scope acts directly, not through an io (exec, push, comment via a CLI). Inert sinks do not make tasks inert. Effects of trial tasks remain open.
