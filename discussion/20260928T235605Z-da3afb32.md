### Where it differs

| | On `verse/ask-next` |
|---|---|
| **G1, one record** | An ask is two records: the raiser's side (`verse.ask.<id>`, a rule's firing, a task) and the question on the docket (`verse.gate.question.<id>`). |
| **G2, changes** | No revision on an ask, an answer or a ballot. A person's ask can't be changed at all (closed). A rule's gate over a world-half subject follows its subject: an edit re-fires it under the same question, and the action runs on the subject as the re-firing saw it. That's the consent gap. |
| **G3, reading** | A rule's gate and a task's question are read by every docket reader. Only a question about a person's ask is read as the ask is. |
| **G4, lifetime** | Only a settled person's ask expires, with its question. A settled rule gate or task question stands. **So does the question of a withdrawn person's ask:** withdrawing removes the ask at once, so it never reaches an expiry. That gap came with 8451a6bc. |
| **G5, resolvers** | Unchanged from main. A resolver owns questions by subject (`resolver.Owner`, applied to every question with a subject and no resolver); the ladder is standing resolvers in name order; a hand-over moves `read-write` (`HoldOut`); `verse gate escalate` re-owns. |
| **G6, the answering side's policy** | None. Anyone may put an ask to anyone, and no resolver overrides an ask's policy. |
| **G7, two ways** | `say` (`POST /tasks/{id}/say`, `POST /flow/say`) is its own path, not an ask. |
| **G8, `then` kinds** | `prompt` is accepted; `calls` and `claude` are not yet. The shape is the model's. |
| **G9, names** | gate, question, ask and rendezvous name overlapping things. |
| **G10, doors** | Command line: raise (`verse ask`), answer (`verse gate`), follow (`verse ask --watch`). Slack posts pending questions to whom they wait on (`gate-ask.tony`) and takes no answers here; answering in Slack is on `verse/slack-llm`. MCP: none. |
| **G11, exact reading** | The permission check lags the store (7q4azhtj). The fix waits on go-tony's `progress` (gkmdvps7, unreleased), in progress in `.worktrees/perms-order`. |
