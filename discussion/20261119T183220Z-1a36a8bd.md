### Decided along the way, beyond the body

- **Uninstall stops a package's work and runs its hooks.** Recorded on 98r85nzvh12ksxsazsn0. A change to a package being uninstalled is refused with `429`, to be asked again.
- **A runner ends a task by a second way of stopping its run** (`Orphan` in `pkg/task/loop.go`), so the frame, the snapshot and the record agree: `failed`, `orphaned: true`, `no reader for <lease>`. A run that finished, or failed for its own reason, keeps its end.
- **For a subprocess task only the parent runner keeps the lease.** It answers a subscribe at once for a task its process does not host yet, since a first clone can outlast a lease.
- **The response deadline on a subscribe** (30s) applies only to verse's mirror, which asks to be told when the runner accepted. Other readers of the stream are not cut.

### Found by the rehearsal, being fixed

On an unsandboxed runner a command the task started outlives the task's process when the runner stops it: the runner ended the task at its lease and its `sleep` ran on. Main leaks the same way at runner shutdown; the lease makes it matter, since the record says the task ended. Under bwrap the sandbox is expected to end the whole tree; that is not run.

The fix, chosen: each task process starts in a process group of its own, and the runner's stop kills what is left of the group. Written with a test that fails without it, under review, not committed.

### Left as it is

- At the 1m floor, two unanswered subscribes outlast the lease. A lease near the largest duration overflows the wait. `observed` flaps on a runner that accepts and then closes.
