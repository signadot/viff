**Typed `GET /asks`: decided with the reviewer (2026-10-23).** Design note: `scratchpad/asks-view.md`, inventory: `scratchpad/asks-inventory.md`.

1. **One record type, two listings.** `api.AskRecord` holds all 56 keys, each optional. There's no type per kind: the record carries no discriminator, a person's ask becomes an onward ask in place, and the kinds share most keys. `AskRecord.Kind()` derives the five kinds (person's ask, onward ask, words to a task, task question, gate question) from `asker`, `task`, `saidTo`, `serves` and `origin`. `resumeAsks` and `deleteAsk` do this by hand today and should use it.
2. **`id` stays on the record.** It's the record's own key. The asker's listing fills it from the address for a record not yet posed, so the asker's row is the record itself, not the record plus `id`.
3. **The writers are made consistent first, in scope:**
   - `answerers` is always a list, and a reader accepts the lone string older records carry (`api.Names`, a curated signature);
   - a failed `waitingOn` read writes nothing instead of `null`;
   - `carried` splits an int `status` from a string `note`.

   The guard was against reshaping storage to fit a type. Two of these are bugs by their own comments, and the third is an inconsistency the CLI already works around. No deployed charter reads any of the three.
4. **The docket stops stripping.** A docket row is the record plus `servedBy` and `where`, with nothing removed. This landed first, on its own, as the fix for 16vj4baeha9xs3d3vdn0 (the git iod's sweep, which read `posedAt` from the docket).

**Follow-ups, not in the asks PR:**
- `about` means two things: the stored first line of `says`, versus the subject's address in `api.Ask`/`api.ServedAsk`. Only a storage rename fixes it; until then it's noted in the type's comment.
- A re-posed question keeps `answer`, `stale` and `orphanedAt`. Once confirmed, that gets its own issue.
