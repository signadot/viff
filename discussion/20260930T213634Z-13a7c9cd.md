**An ask has an `else`** (2026-09-30: "we have ask … then but not ask … then … else for no good reason").
- **When it runs:** on a no, whether answered or by silence settling on no at the date.
- **What it takes:** what `then` takes, on the asker's authority alone.
- **Withdrawn or orphaned:** runs neither.
- **Changing it** asks again, as a new edit.
- **What it's for:** escalation is one ask, `--else '{ask: {…, answerers: [lead]}}'`.
