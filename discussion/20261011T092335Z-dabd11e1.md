## The shape, as I read that direction

A runner's tools already have one vocabulary: `harness.Tool`, a name, a description, a JSON-schema of parameters, a `Derive` that stamps effects, and a `Do` that performs it in the task process. That vocabulary is also what an MCP tool is (name, description, inputSchema, call → content) and what codex's `dynamicTools` is (name, description, inputSchema, `item/tool/call` → content). The loop's handling of a call is the same under every presentation: derive, gate, perform, report. So the thing to organise is one toolset with several presentations, and the toolset is the runner's.

Three steps, each one usable on its own:

1. **Bootstrap, on `verse/codex`.** The codex driver offers the task's runner tools (today: `push`) through `dynamicTools` on `thread/start`, and turns each `item/tool/call` into an action the loop gates, the tool's `Derive` classifying it and its `Do` performing it, the output going back as the call's content. The finisher goes, under both drivers. The brief tells the agent to commit what it wants delivered, with the trailers, and to call `push`; the push tool checks the trailers and refuses with the reason. The glossary demo then shows the agent deciding to deliver, and the question on the docket is the agent's `push`, not the runner's.

2. **Organise.** Lift the tool type out of `harness` into a package of its own (`task/tool`, say), so it is nobody's driver's. Three presentations beside it: the harness's function-calling adapter (exists), the codex `dynamicTools` adapter (from step 1), and room for a third. The environment's `tools:` floor applies to every driver, not only `llm`. A runner then declares its toolset once (`push` now; `ask` and whatever a runner adds later) and every driver presents it.

3. **Later, if it goes that way: the verse-runner MCP.** The same toolset served over MCP by the task process, which codex (and any MCP-speaking agent) consumes natively; `dynamicTools` becomes one presentation among others, or goes. Nothing in steps 1 and 2 has to change for that, because the toolset and the gate path are already the parts that would be served.

Not in scope now: moving `ask` to codex through the bridge (falls out of step 2, decide then); the agent-side of a third runner.
