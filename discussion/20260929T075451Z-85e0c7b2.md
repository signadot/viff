**For stage 4 (2026-09-29): conventional labels.** Asks may carry conventional labels (`labels:` or the like), so a resolver's match can say what kind of ask it follows (`review`, `release`, `deploy-approval`, …) without routing and without reading the words. A raiser labels an ask. A rule's gate can label its asks in the charter, and a task's question through its tool. A resolver matches on labels beside `about`. Which labels are conventional is a small shared vocabulary, like a git-issue label. Folded into stage 4's match design; not built.
