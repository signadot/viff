`deny` on a task's gate belongs here, not to the cut (Scott, 2026-10-18). The spawn spec has no
`deny`: a gate asks, and its deadline refuses. "Never push main" needs none — it is the absence
of `main` in the repo's allowance, and the runner refuses such a push before any gate. What
`deny` is for is the steps a runner intercepts inside the run (commands.run, files.write,
network.fetch), and the shape it takes is a row of the policy list: `{match, then: deny}`,
first match wins, `then: ask` the default so every gate written today reads unchanged. The
runner's ceiling is the same list with no `ask`.
