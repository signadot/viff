**Step 0, 2026-11-07: the staging pod.** A throwaway pod on staging (namespace `pkgnix-try`) with the runner's image and settings (`hostUsers: false`, `procMount: Unmasked`, seccomp `Unconfined`, uid 65532, no capabilities) on a t3.large, Bottlerocket, kernel 6.18.54, and a 30Gi gp3 claim. The report, the scripts and the logs are attached as `pkgnix-pod/`. The body is revised for what it found.

**Holds:**
- Wrap's flags under `hostUsers: false`, with bwrap 0.8.0 and 0.13.1; `--remount-ro /` refuses `/` and leaves the workspace and `/tmp` writable.
- The store apart (`nix --store /state/nix`) on the claim: 11 packages by `meta.outputsToInstall`, 471 paths, 4.2 GB from cache.nixos.org in 52 s. Bound to `/nix/store`, every program runs from a `PATH` of 13 `bin` directories; the store refuses writes; `/state` is not there.
- Binds with 0.13.1: 45 ms at 471, 216 ms at 2,980, linear (about 70 µs each, 5–7 times local). 0.8.0: 9.8 s at 2,980. The 9000-argument cap is the same. Command line and `--args` cost the same.
- Roots in the store apart: realise-and-root in one command keeps a closure through a collection; unrooted paths go; a running task keeps its paths with no root.
- Concurrency: two realises at once both complete; a collection during a realise takes nothing of it and does not wait for it.
- pure-eval refuses a file of the runner's; `accept-flake-config = false` ignores a flake's `nixConfig`; nix honours `github:`'s `host` (it asked `example.invalid`), so the parser's refusal is needed.

**Changes the plan:**
1. **`restrict-eval` refuses a flake's own inputs** ("access to URI 'github:NixOS/nixpkgs/…' is forbidden in restricted mode"). With `allowed-uris` naming them it evaluates, an input outside is refused, and the match is by path segment (`github:NixOS/nix` does not admit `github:NixOS/nixpkgs`). So a new `nix.sources`, written as `allowed-uris`, bounds the inputs too.
2. **`trusted-settings.json` overrides `accept-flake-config = false`:** a saved yes applied a flake's extra cache and key. The runner removes the file at its start and before each evaluation.
3. **Two of the 471 store paths are symlinks** into `/nix/store/…`, which names nothing on the runner's side: `--ro-bind` failed ("Can't find source path"). The sandbox recreates them with `--symlink`.
4. **No nixpkgs ships bwrap 0.13** (pinned, `nixpkgs-unstable`, `nixos-unstable`: 0.12.0). From source, then; open question 4 is settled.
5. **Evaluation:** cold 36–41 s and 578 MiB, warm **3.2 s** (not 0.2 s) and 462 MiB: a list through `--apply` gets no evaluation cache. The runner caches its answer by the locked reference; the memory bound is 1 GiB.
6. **nix needs a `HOME` its uid owns** (65532 has no passwd entry); **the claim needs `fsGroup: 65532`**, which the runner pod does not set today.
7. **Roots of several paths** are named `<name>-1`, `<name>-2`: removed by the task's prefix.

Not settled: a store on a node the pod moves off, a real acme or private flake, a whole launch through runnerd.

The pod is still up for a re-run: `kubectl --context my-cluster.example.ts.net delete namespace pkgnix-try` removes it and its claim.
