Proposed vocabulary.

- **resolver**: the record at `verse.resolvers.<kind>.<name>`, verse's manifestation of whoever answers, the way a charter is. `resolver.Resolver` is already this type and `verse resolver add|show|remove` already operates on it, so the rename is prose and helpers rather than the type or the CLI.
- **resolver service**: the process a `url` resolver hands streams to. Only that driver has one; a `person` or `group` resolver's person or members answer from the docket as themselves, so "service" appears exactly where the old ambiguity was ("the service dials in").
- **resolver instance**: unchanged, the per-run record of a resolver service reading one run.

The verb goes with the noun. "Bind", "bound" and "unbind" are as vague as "binding"; the question record already names its owner `resolver:`, so "the question's resolver" and "assigned to a resolver" replace "bound to a binding". `mirror.Bind` becomes something like `mirror.Assign`.

Only the registration sense moves. The other senses of "binding" stay: variables bound into a spawn (`launch.Binding`, `payloadBinding`, `bindText`, `bindCalls`, `checkNoBinding`), TCP binds (`up.go`, `docd.go`) and the CLI's filter binding (`main.go`, `filter.go`). Search and replace is wrong here.

What breaks is small: the `binding:` field on instance records (per run, re-minted on restart), refusal messages and CLI synopses. `via:verse.resolvers.<kind>.<name>` is unchanged. The grantee grammar added uses of the old word that go with this rename: `Grammar()`'s "a rule, a source or a binding acting" and the principals section of docs/perms.md.

A collision this does not resolve: `task.Resolver` (`task.AutoResolver`) is the runtime's in-process gate answerer, a different thing under the same name. It is internal Go and rarely in prose, so it can stay; note it so it is not renamed by accident.
