A minimal fix against main, independent of `verse/remove-broker`: `verse/broker-local` in `.worktrees/broker-local`, one commit `7be34cdc` on main `cd336711`, unpushed, declared `Tier: contract` with `Breaks:` for `--runner …/broker/local`.

What it does: `brokerFor` no longer maps the fixed name `local` to `Taskd.Handler()`. Every path under `/broker/<nonce>` is a handler `HostBroker` registered under a minted nonce, or 404. The built-in supervisor is called in process and has no URL. `docs/task-control.md`, `docs/seams.md` and the `up` comment stop naming `/broker/local`, and task-control.md says why the mount is gone.

On the three things this issue asked to settle before closing:

- **the resolve bypass**: moot and now pinned. `TestTheLocalRuntimeIsNotServedOnTheBrokerFace` posts to `/broker/local/tasks`, `/tasks/x/resolve`, `/tasks/x/cancel` and `/tasks/x/vector` on the broker face and expects 404 for each and an empty runtime after. Nothing under the fixed name reaches `d.resolve` any more, so the question of what it would have answered does not arise.
- **does anybody use `--runner …/broker/local`**: not asked, not counted; nothing in the tree dials it, and the `Breaks:` trailer says what a binding has to do instead.
- **what replaced it**: another verse that wants this one's runtime installs it as a runner by the operator URL, where `runner.HTTP` meets the door. That path's own gap (the http runner sending no `Verse-Principal`) is this issue's option 3 and stays open as its own thing.

Closes when the branch lands. `verse/remove-broker` (hahx7ddc) removes the whole face and will meet a trivial conflict in `versed/server.go` on rebase, since the mount is gone there too.
