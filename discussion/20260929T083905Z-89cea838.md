**Decided (2026-09-29), D3, stage 3: an ask's reading defers to what it is about.** The subject's readers can't be listed (ownership above, inherited grants, groups, kinds), so they can't be copied onto the ask's record. Instead the ask's perms record says its reading defers to its subject, and the read check follows that link: whoever may read the subject may read the ask, and its parties read it as they answer it. This applies to a rule's gate and a task's question that have a subject. Rejected:
- copying the subject's own `read` line at pose, which misses what's inherited;
- leaving these asks readable by the whole docket, which leaks the subject's words.
