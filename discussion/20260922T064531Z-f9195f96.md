A request can no longer present a binding's address as its principal: only `user:`, `agent:` or `runner:` are accepted, so the spelling in §3 is refused 400. Installing a binding is governed by its owner record since 0c807e51. The gap that remains in §3 is that a `user:` or `agent:` name is self-asserted, which is authority's subject. §2, §4, §5 and §6 stand.
