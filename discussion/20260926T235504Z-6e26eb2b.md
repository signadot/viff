A SECOND FIELD, same two writers: `resolver`. Fixed as far as `status` is, `16198e7f`.

Seen 2026-09-27 in a whole-repo `go test` on `verse/ask-next` (base 0219de31):

    --- FAIL: TestRemovingTheLastResolverSendsAQuestionToTheDocket (0.14s)
        standing_test.go:458: owned by verse.resolvers.alpha after its only resolver went, want nobody

Passes alone (5/5), in whole-package `./versed` runs on the branch and on main, and 150/150 alone under competing `go test` load.

**The mechanism.** The frame's post (`PostFrameQuestion`) wrote the owner it looked up when the frame was reported — `resolver: <owner>` — UNCONDITIONALLY, and held the question out to it (`holdOutAgain`). Removing a resolver hands its questions on (`reown`, which stamps `reownedFrom` and clears `resolver`). A frame post whose owner was computed before the removal and whose write lands after it names the removed resolver again, with its grant: a question stranded on a name nothing answers, though the removal did everything right. `status` was made conditional in 7b6cb09d; `resolver` is the same kind of field — one a later act contradicts — and was left out.

**Reproduced deliberately**: a 300ms sleep between the frame path's owner lookup and its post (`mirror.go`, the `EvPending` case), and the test reading one second after the removal instead of instantly: 3/3 failing with the exact message above; 3/3 passing without the sleep; 3/3 passing with the sleep after the fix. (A sleep alone with the test's instant read passes — the late write lands after the assertion — which is why two earlier probes looked like exonerations.)

**The fix**: the owner lands only on a question with no `reownedFrom` (a whole-payload `!not {reownedFrom: <present>}`, as `notStatus` is spelled), and the grant only when that write lands. Regression: `TestALateFrameDoesNotNameAResolverTheQuestionWasHandedOnFrom` delivers the late frame by hand after the handover, and fails with the condition removed (the field) and with the grant unconditional (the grant). Same caveat as `status`: a precondition checked against a view that can lag narrows the window, it does not close it — the two writers still want ORDERING, which is what this issue stays open for.
