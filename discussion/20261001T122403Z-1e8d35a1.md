**Decided: refs drive the lifecycle, not branch contents.** (2026-10-01)

- **What runs in verse is decided by a tag.** The newest `live-verse.N` tag names the tree that is installed. It normally sits on main, but presence on main is not what decides; the tag is. Area 1 (j9vbwvja) therefore keeps the newest `live-verse.N` installed, not main's sha. Area 3 (jhvbwvja) ends with verse merging *and* tagging. A rollback is a new tag on an older commit.
- **The review environment's lifecycle is decided by a branch naming convention.** A branch matching the convention opens a review environment (area 2, jdvbwvja). Deleting or merging it closes the environment. This fits the trial's tag lifecycle (`verse-sandbox-<nonce>`, area 4).
