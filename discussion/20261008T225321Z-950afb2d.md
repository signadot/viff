2026-10-09: `verse disconnect` is removed with `verse connect` (verse/remove-connect, 26e41eb0, issue h6hx7ddc). The body's "What is built" names `disconnect -scope <name>` as the drop; what remains is the route alone, `POST /scope/delete?scope=<name>`, which stops the scope's sources and engine, cancels its live tasks and runs logd's `DeleteScope`. No CLI verb reaches it. So the surface this issue proposes (list, get, delete, optionally create) is also where the CLI's drop would come back, as the delete half of a scope surface rather than as a verb of its own; until then a scope is made by any scoped write and dropped only by the route.
