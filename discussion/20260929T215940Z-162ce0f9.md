**Naming (2026-09-29).** `verse gate` became `verse question`, the answerer's side, as `verse ask` is the asker's. It's named in the singular, as every collection is, with aliases `questions`, `qs`, `gate` and `gates`.
- **The listing** shows the questions waiting on you: `waitingOn` names you and you didn't raise it. `--all` shows the whole docket.
- **The watch splits:** `ask --watch` covers your asks, and `question --watch` covers what waits on you.
- **Words:** a question is an ask as the person asked sees it; the docket is every pending ask you can read; a gate is the charter's checkpoint.
