The ordering, attempt 1: built, not committed (worktree `.worktrees/perms-order`, uncommitted; measurements in `scratchpad/measure_permsorder_test.go.txt`). It stopped short of the bar, "fails closed, and doesn't make every read pay a mirror read".

**Built**
- A watermark per view: the lower of the records watch and the root-record watch.
- A barrier: one read of `verse.perms` that reports its commit, applied to the view and shared by coalesced waiters.
- Failing closed on barrier error or timeout.
- Watch commits say the revision their changes were taken against, and whether they came from a re-opened watch.
- `Get`, `ReadAt`, both listings and `PathsAt` report the commit they were served at.

**Tests,** each failing with its piece removed:
- stranger read: 0/50 admitted, 49/50 on main;
- stranger's watch: nothing delivered;
- revoke, read and watch: refused at once;
- stalled view: refused;
- routes, with the record written through `Establish`: 0 admitted via `GET /entity` and `GET /watch`, 30/30 on main.

**Why it misses the bar**

| load | stranger `Get` p50 | plain `Get` p50 | barriers |
|---|---|---|---|
| idle | 308µs | 259µs | 1 per 500 reads |
| ~68 writes/s | 260µs (p99 4.2ms) | 215µs | 22 / 500 |
| ~228 writes/s | 3.9ms | 240µs | 499 / 500 |
| ~365 writes/s | 4.2ms | 290µs | 500 / 500 |

A read's R is the store's head, which moves with every write. Nothing marks the view current past revisions that carry no perms change (logd sends no progress events), so under steady writes every read waits for a barrier, about 4.1ms.

**The missing piece** is a signal that the view is current through R. The candidates:
1. A watch on the whole root that reads every commit and conservatively checks whether it touched perms. The step 0 probe showed even a root watch skips some commits (a same-value write, a scope), which would still need a barrier.
2. logd reporting how far each watch is current: a progress event, or a "delivered through C" on each event. That's a go-tony change.
