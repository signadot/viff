**Decided, 2026-11-07: the bases are prepared at the runner's start, ahead of any launch** (open question 5, as the review proposed). Folded into step 5, step 6's order, the tests, and open question 5, which now asks only about a spawn's flake.

- **At the start, after the collection** (step 6: stale task roots, then roots of bases no doer names, then the collection, then the bases), every base is evaluated, realised and rooted. A launch then pays only its flake's evaluation and substitution.
- **The runner serves while they are prepared.** A launch whose base is not ready waits for it, up to an evaluation's timeout, rather than repeating the work or being refused.
- **A base is locked once** when prepared, and holds until a restart or a configuration naming another. The locked reference is logged and recorded with each task. The docs say to name a `rev`.
- **A base that fails** is logged, and prepared again by the next launch that needs it; a launch whose base fails again fails before any process. A network blip at the start closes nothing for good.
- **The evaluation's answer is kept on the store's volume,** by locked reference and system, so a restart with the same base does not evaluate it (3 s warm, about 40 s cold, otherwise).

Left open (question 5): a spawn's flake costs nothing if evaluated before, about 3 s over a nixpkgs the store has, about 40 s over one it has not. Whether to prepare the ceiling's flakes ahead too waits on the first users' launches.
