## Decided (2026-10-31): where the pacing lives, and what pending is

**One gate, in the store.** The constraint is logd's 8 reads per session, and the session is `LogdStore`'s, so the gate is there: `establish` takes a slot, and the pump releases it when the watch's first State is seeded (or the establish fails). Every watch verse opens passes through it: a rule arming, a re-establish after a drop, a source's slice. The reconciler does no pacing of its own; arming a batch blocks on the gate. Start at 4 slots, under logd's 8 so pings, writes and ordinary reads keep flowing; measure before moving it. **FIFO** at the gate: no priority for re-establishes over new installs' watches. A `--wait` behind a round of reopens waits, and status says why.

**Pending is held-with-cause**, built on `place`'s hold (trigger_api.go:323-350, `e.pending`): a held rule stands by name in the charter today, waiting on its stratum. The record gains a cause, and `vet`'s refusals split:
- *hold*: stratum missing; no runner offers the spawn; a declaration or the perms mirror could not be read; the watch did not open (today's `arming`);
- *refuse*: condition, action or gate `validate`; an action that does nothing; a credential without a gate; gates written twice; reads outside a declaration that was read.

**Re-vet on what could clear a cause**: a runner registration, a declaration write, a session reconnect; and a **one-minute timer** as the backstop. The re-vet is against in-memory state; the only read is a runner's reachability where that is the cause. `systemWaiting` (versed/server.go:4031) folds into the same list.

**Two stages.** Stage A is the gate alone, filed on its own, built first and measured: a forced drop on a local verse with ~400 watches (`subtreeReopens` rate, heartbeat timeouts), then staging's next restart. Stage B, this issue: held-with-cause, the re-vet triggers, `--wait` (the earlier comment), and status.
