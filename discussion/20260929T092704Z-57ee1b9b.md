**Stage 4 decisions** (the stage-4 prep reconciled with the model; recommendations accepted):

- **A match** covers what an ask is about, plus conventional `labels:`. It does not route, so there are no `to`/`raiser` dimensions, and Q1 dissolves: a person's resolver is a resolver holding that person's grant.
- **Answer-for** is an `answer` level. A place grants it at the place, and a person at `verse.for.<person>`. Resolver R's answer counts for party P only when:
  - R serves the ask;
  - P holds read-write on the ask;
  - `via:R` holds `answer` at P's node.

  The ballot is P's, with `via: R`.
- **Q2, overlap:** refused only where two resolvers could answer for the same party on the same ask: their matches overlap, and they hold `answer` from one granter. It is checked at install and at grant time. A match alone costs nobody anything, so claiming one first gains nothing.
- **Q3:** a party served by a resolver still answers for themselves. Overriding that is stage 5.
- **Q4:** the person and group drivers are retired. Every resolver is a service; a declarative `forward:` comes when something needs it.
- **Q5:** only the resolver serving an ask links an onward ask to it.
- **Q6:** versed always carries an onward ask's answer back.
