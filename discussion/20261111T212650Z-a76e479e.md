Prior writing, found after filing: `docs/articles/external-tools-and-secrets.md` on `verse/pkg` (section "stdio servers, through `secret-exec`'s sandbox") already describes "the runner is the client" for a stdio server: a `command` kind of server in `mcp`'s configuration, with `secret` and `as`, started by the `Confiner`, each call an `mcp.call`. This issue agrees with it on the model and the transport.

It differs in one place. The article has the server run as a `secret.exec` command does, over the task's workspace and under the task's network. The body above has it see no workspace by default and name its own network. That difference is open question 4 and the network point, and the article's reading is the cheaper one to build: the Confiner as it is.

The article does not take up the two questions this issue was filed for: how such a server is named in the offer, and whether its flags are gated.
